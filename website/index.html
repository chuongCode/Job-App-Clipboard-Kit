Job App Clipboard Kit keeps the information you reuse in one compact Firefox extension. Open it over an application, select a saved detail, and paste.

Personal
Names, phone, email, address, and location
Experience
Positions, companies, dates, and descriptions
Links
Portfolio, LinkedIn, and additional URLs
Education
Schools, degrees, disciplines, and dates

Your profile stays in Firefox.

No account, backend, analytics, or third-party runtime. Profile data is stored with Firefox extension storage on your device.
